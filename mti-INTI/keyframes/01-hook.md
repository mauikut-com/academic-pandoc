# Scene 01 — Hook (keyframes)

## Start frame

Still image, 16:9. High-quality 3D animated explainer in the style of Zack D
Films. Wide shot of a dark navy void with soft volumetric light rays. Three
stylized 3D products — a white milk carton with a blue "Cimory" band, a white
yogurt cup, and a red-and-gold ready-to-eat sausage pack — are just beginning
to orbit a large glowing smartphone floating center-screen, thin cyan light
trails starting to trace their paths. The phone shows a clean Cimory
storefront. No caption yet. Glossy 3D CGI, neon cyan accents, subtle depth of
field, cinematic lighting. No motion blur.

## End frame

Still image, 16:9. Same style and scene, but the camera has pushed in close to
the phone screen, the three products are mid-orbit with full cyan light trails,
and a bold white caption reads "Can your sales app survive a flash sale?" A
faint Cimory logo glows behind the phone. Glossy 3D CGI, dark navy background,
crisp legible caption text.

## Negative

photorealistic, live action, real products, film grain, blurry, low detail,
text artifacts, misspelled words, any logo other than Cimory, cluttered
composition, harsh flat lighting, washed-out colors
