# 00 — Look-dev (style lock)

One hero frame that establishes the full look and palette. Use it as the
style reference for every scene keyframe.

## Prompt

Still image, 16:9. High-quality 3D animated explainer in the style of Zack D
Films. Dark navy and charcoal void with soft volumetric light rays and a
subtle vignette. Center-frame: a stylized smartphone showing a clean Cimory
storefront (milk carton, yogurt cup, and sausage pack tiles). Orbiting the
phone are three stylized 3D products — a white milk carton with a blue "Cimory"
band, a white yogurt cup, and a red-and-gold ready-to-eat sausage pack — with
thin cyan light trails behind them. To one side, a floating translucent
holographic data panel shows a green "0.8 s" readout, and a small orange
warning triangle, together displaying the full color palette: neon cyan and
electric blue for health, warm orange for warnings. Clean glossy 3D CGI, subtle
depth of field, cinematic lighting. Crisp, legible text. No motion blur, no
watermark.

## Negative

photorealistic, live action, real products, film grain, blurry, low detail,
text artifacts, misspelled words, extra fingers, any logo other than Cimory,
cluttered composition, harsh flat lighting, washed-out colors, no motion
