# Scene 05 — Scale-down (keyframes)

## Start frame

Still image, 16:9. High-quality 3D animated explainer in the style of Zack D
Films. A row of eight glowing server racks in a dark navy data center. The
first several racks have just powered off: their lights are faded, cabinets
dimmed, and they are beginning to retract into the dark background, leaving
only a few racks still lit. Moody warm-red lighting begins to creep in. Glossy
3D CGI, neon accents. No motion blur.

## End frame

Still image, 16:9. Only one server rack remains, glowing red and overloaded.
In the foreground, a stylized smartphone held by a customer avatar shows the
Cimory checkout screen with a spinning loader. Above the phone, a holographic
timer reads "5 minutes", and it is flashing "timeout". Neighboring customer
avatars show red exclamation marks while their carts fade away. A bold caption
reads "Scale down, and the slowdown trickles down to every user — in minutes."
Moody warm-red lighting, dark navy background, glossy 3D CGI, crisp legible
caption and timer text.

## Negative

photorealistic, live action, real human faces, film grain, blurry, low detail,
text artifacts, misspelled words, any logo other than Cimory, new servers
appearing, green or recovery indicators, a sudden jump (degradation must read
as gradual)
