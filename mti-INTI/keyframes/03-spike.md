# Scene 03 — Spike (keyframes)

## Start frame

Still image, 16:9. High-quality 3D animated explainer in the style of Zack D
Films. A floating holographic flash-sale countdown reads "3 … 2 … 1" in large
white digits. In the background, thousands of tiny glowing user avatars begin
to stream toward a single server rack in a dark navy data center. The rack
still shows cyan status lights, just starting to warm. Glossy 3D CGI, floating
holographic UI, cinematic lighting. No motion blur.

## End frame

Still image, 16:9. The single server rack now glows red and orange with a heat
shimmer, its status lights flickering. Yellow warning triangles pop in around
it. Glowing order packets pile into a large, crowded queue in front of the
rack, and a holographic latency readout shows a high, climbing number. Dramatic
warm lighting against the dark navy background. Glossy 3D CGI, crisp legible
readout text.

## Negative

photorealistic, live action, real people, film grain, blurry, low detail, text
artifacts, misspelled words, any logo other than Cimory, additional servers,
green recovery indicators, an empty queue
