# Scene 07 — Outro (keyframes)

## Start frame

Still image, 16:9. High-quality 3D animated explainer in the style of Zack D
Films. The Cimory storefront smartphone sits front and center with a steady
green status light. The three stylized products — milk carton, yogurt cup, and
RTE sausage pack — are settling into place beside it. The Cimory logo is just
beginning to fade in above. Calm cyan lighting, soft volumetric glow, glossy 3D
CGI, dark navy background. No motion blur.

## End frame

Still image, 16:9. Final composition: the phone front and center with a steady
green status light, the three products neatly arranged beside it, the Cimory
logo fully visible above, and a caption reading "Scale smart. Keep every order
fast." Calm cyan lighting, glossy 3D CGI, dark navy background, crisp legible
caption and logo.

photorealistic, live action, real products, film grain, blurry, low detail,
